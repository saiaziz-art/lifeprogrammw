Sequencing

What's actually active across the whole programme, phase by phase.

Yr 1–2
  • Family PlanningHave the timing conversation and align on a target window.
  • Savings / DepositsStanding order live; weekly review habit established.
  • Health + FitnessMarathon base + build phases; half marathon tune-up race.
  • CommunityRebuild the weekly anchor; join one club for the autumn term.
  • Adventure & TravelPortugal and Japan trips banked and taken.
  • HobbiesGuitar habit running; photo series shot and finished.
Yr 2–3
  • Savings / DepositsFund crosses roughly the halfway point.
  • Health + FitnessPeak marathon block, then race day.
  • Side HustleReactivation review against parked criteria.
  • Long-Term + Short-Term GoalsYr 2 structural decision review — location and home project.
  • Learning & DevelopmentCPD and personal technical practice continue as steady background load.
Yr 3–4
  • Savings / DepositsApproaching the deposit target; scenario re-checked against real rates.
  • Long-Term + Short-Term GoalsHome project go/no-go executed.
  • Health, Learning, Hobbies, CommunityHeld steady as ongoing maintenance, not active projects.
Yr 4–6
  • Long-Term + Short-Term GoalsRevisit the "life at 40" vision document.
  • Whole ProgrammeFull review of every work package's status, cadence, and sequencing.
  • Side HustleReassess dormancy honestly — reactivate, extend, or retire the idea bank.
09

Non-Negotiables

Genuinely few, genuinely non-negotiable. These override everything above when they conflict.

  1. Family time and health commitments are never sacrificed to hit a savings rate.
  2. No side hustle activity starts until the deposit fund and marathon training are both on track.
  3. One full rest day every week — no exceptions, no "just this once."
  4. Every bonus or windfall goes to the deposit fund before it goes anywhere else.
  5. The family planning timing decision is not deferred past the Yr 1 review.
